Typical challenges in BMS projects

Four topics that often cost time in BMS projects.

State of charge with LFP cells

ChallengeIn the flat region of the curve, the open-circuit voltage (OCV) of an LFP cell typically changes by less than one millivolt per percent of state of charge, plus hysteresis that depends on cell type, temperature and history. OCV-based correction barely works, and coulomb counting drifts with every current-sensor offset.

Our approachModel-based state estimators, such as adaptive Kalman filters, tuned on real driving and charging profiles. We use rest phases and the steeper parts of the curve at low and high state of charge for recalibration, where relaxation time and temperature allow a reliable correction.

BMS software development (DE)

Contactors, precharge and fault reaction

ChallengeA welded contactor, an aborted precharge, insulation resistance below the UN R100 limit of 100 Ω/V for DC circuits: the critical moments are rarely the nominal states but the transitions between driving, charging and shutdown.

Our approachWe design mode and contactor management from the failure cases and, during design, define for every transition the safe state and the fault tolerant time interval (FTTI) within which it must be reached.

Contactor and mode management (DE)

ISO 26262 safety evidence

ChallengeFor ASIL D safety goals, ISO 26262 requires hardware architectural metrics of at least 99 % (SPFM) and 90 % (LFM). If the necessary diagnostics are defined late, iterations in hardware and software follow.

Our approachWe define diagnostics, safety mechanisms and the hardware-software interface (HSI) alongside the system design, so the safety case grows with the development.

Functional safety (DE)

Electrifying agricultural and construction machinery

ChallengeA tractor works for hours under high continuous load, sits idle for months and is exposed to dust, moisture and vibration throughout. Cell technology from cars often carries over; the operating strategy does not. For agricultural and forestry machinery, ISO 25119 is often the relevant functional safety standard; for other machines and functions, ISO 13849 or ISO 19014 may apply depending on the risk assessment.

Our approachWe keep what fits from automotive and adapt what the machine demands: derating for continuous load, deep-discharge protection for long idle periods and a safety concept based on the machine's risk assessment (AgPL or PL).

What keeps BMS teams busy in 2026 and 2027

Several regulations set new requirements for state estimation and fault reaction.

1 Jul 2026

China: GB 38031-2025

In force since 1 July 2026: in the prescribed propagation test the battery system must neither catch fire nor explode, and a timely warning is required. This rests on cells, barriers, venting and mechanics; the BMS contributes detection, warning and fault reaction.

29 Nov 2026

Euro 7: battery durability

New passenger-car types must retain 80 % of certified energy after five years or 100,000 km and 72 % after eight years or 160,000 km. Determining the state of certified energy (SOCE) becomes relevant for type approval.

20 Jan 2027

EU Machinery Regulation

Software with a safety function and protection against corruption feed into the conformity assessment, depending on the safety function also for the battery system of electric construction and self-propelled agricultural machinery. Tractors fall under EU type approval (Regulation (EU) 167/2013).

18 Feb 2027

EU battery passport

Mandatory for EV batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh. State-of-health data comes from the BMS.
