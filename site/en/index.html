Where experience makes the difference

Four core topics in BMS development and how we approach them

State of charge with LFP cells

ChallengeIn the flat region of the curve, the rest voltage of an LFP cell typically changes by less than 1 mV per percent of state of charge, plus hysteresis. OCV correction barely works, coulomb counting drifts.

Our approachModel-based estimators such as adaptive Kalman filters, tuned on real driving and charging profiles. We use rest phases and steep parts of the curve for recalibration.

BMS software development (DE)

Contactors, precharge and fault reaction

ChallengeWelded contactor, aborted precharge, insulation fault: the critical moments are rarely the nominal states but the transitions between driving, charging and shutdown.

Our approachWe specify normal operation and failure cases together. For every safety-relevant fault, detection and safe state are defined and verified against the fault tolerant time interval (FTTI).

Contactor and mode management (DE)

ISO 26262 safety evidence

ChallengeFor ASIL D safety goals, ISO 26262 requires hardware metrics of at least 99 % (SPFM) and 90 % (LFM). If diagnostics are defined late, iterations in hardware and software follow.

Our approachWe define diagnostics, safety mechanisms and HSI alongside the system design. The safety case grows with the development.

Functional safety (DE)

Electrifying agricultural and construction machinery

ChallengeA tractor works for hours under continuous load and sits idle for months, in dust, moisture and vibration. Cells often carry over from cars, the operating strategy does not.

Our approachDerating for continuous load, deep-discharge protection for long idle periods and a safety concept based on the machine's risk assessment.

Current developments

Several regulations set new requirements for state estimation, fault reaction and software updates

1 Jul 2026

China: GB 38031-2025

In force since 1 July 2026: in the propagation test the battery system must neither catch fire nor explode, and a timely warning is required. The BMS provides detection, warning and fault reaction.

29 Nov 2026

Euro 7: battery durability

New passenger-car types must retain 80 % of certified energy after five years or 100,000 km and 72 % after eight years or 160,000 km. SOCE determination becomes relevant for type approval.

20 Jan 2027

EU Machinery Regulation

Software with a safety function and protection against corruption feed into the conformity assessment, depending on the function also for the battery system of electric work machines.

18 Feb 2027

EU battery passport

Mandatory for EV batteries and industrial batteries above 2 kWh. State-of-health data comes from the BMS.

Ongoing

Software-defined vehicle

Since July 2024, UN R155 (cybersecurity) and R156 (software updates) apply to all new vehicles in the EU. Every BMS update must be secure, traceable and covered by the safety case.
